# Tarteel Handout: Tajweed at a Glance

*A one-stop summary of the course. Print it, keep it beside your Mushaf, and practice with a teacher.*

Course site: <https://husseinbenz.github.io/tarteel/> · Anki decks: on the site's home page.

---

## 1. How to study

1. **Learn one lesson** (about 10 minutes): meet the rule, read the examples aloud, press play.
2. **Do the flashcards** for that lesson. Say the fragment out loud *before* you flip.
3. **Review daily** in the Flashcards section or in Anki (5 to 10 minutes).
4. **Recite to your teacher.** Tajweed is learned by listening and being corrected.

> Counts below are steady beats, about one finger movement each. They follow the common teaching for Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim. If your teacher differs, follow your teacher.

## 2. The color key

| Color | Rule | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Light blue | Natural madd | Stretch 2 counts |
| Violet | Madd on stopping / leen | Stretch 2, 4 or 6 counts when you stop |
| Royal blue | Connected & separated madd | Stretch 4 to 5 counts |
| Deep navy | Necessary madd | Stretch 6 counts |
| Red | Qalqalah | A small echo on ق ط ب ج د |
| Orange | Ghunnah | Nasal hum, 2 counts, on نّ مّ |
| Purple | Ikhfa | Hide the noon, hum 2 counts |
| Pink | Ikhfa shafawi | Hide the meem before ب, hum 2 counts |
| Teal | Idgham with ghunnah | Merge, with a 2-count hum |
| Green | Idgham without ghunnah | Merge, no hum |
| Lime | Idgham shafawi | Merge meem into meem |
| Sky blue | Iqlab | Noon becomes a hidden meem |
| Gold / brown | Merging two letters | Merge similar or close letters |
| Gray | Silent or hidden | Connecting alif, silent lam, silent letters |

**Longer madd = deeper blue.**

## 3. Noon sakinah (نْ) and tanween: decided by the next letter

| Next letter | Rule | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| ء ه ع ح غ خ (throat) | **Idhhar** | Say the noon clearly |
| ي ن م و | **Idgham with ghunnah** | Merge, hum 2 counts |
| ل ر | **Idgham without ghunnah** | Merge, no hum |
| ب | **Iqlab** | Hidden meem, hum 2 counts |
| the other 15: ت ث ج د ذ ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ف ق ك | **Ikhfa** | Hide, hum 2 counts |

Memory aids: **يرملون** (yarmaloon) for idgham. Four words keep a clear noon: دُنْيَا، بُنْيَـٰن، صِنْوَان، قِنْوَان.

## 4. Meem sakinah (مْ)

| Next letter | Rule | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| ب | **Ikhfa shafawi** | Lips lightly touching, hum 2 counts |
| م | **Idgham shafawi** | One doubled meem, hum 2 counts |
| anything else | **Idhhar shafawi** | Clear meem. Take extra care before و and ف |

---

## 5. Madd: how long?

| Type | Cause | Counts |
|---|---|---|
| **Natural** (طبيعي) | madd letter, nothing special after | **2** |
| **Badal** (بدل) | hamza *before* the madd letter | **2** |
| **Connected** (متصل) | hamza *after* the madd letter, same word | **4 to 5** |
| **Separated** (منفصل) | word ends in madd letter, next starts with hamza | **4 to 5** |
| **On stopping** (عارض) | madd letter before the final letter you stop on | **2, 4 or 6** |
| **Leen** (لين) | waw/yaa with sukun after fathah, stopping | **2, 4 or 6** |
| **Necessary** (لازم) | madd letter + sukun/shaddah in the same word | **6** |
| **Silah** (صلة) | pronoun هُ / هِ between two vowelled letters | **2** (or **4 to 5** before a hamza) |

Madd letters (word to remember: نُوحِيهَا): alif after fathah, waw after dammah, yaa after kasrah.

## 6. Letter rules

- **Qalqalah**: ق ط ب ج د (*qutbu jadd*) with a sukun, or when you stop on them. Minor in the middle, major at a stop.
- **Sun letters** (lam is silent, next letter doubled): ت ث د ذ ر ز س ش ص ض ط ظ ل ن
- **Moon letters** (lam is clear): ا ب ج ح خ ع غ ف ق ك م ه و ي
- **Hamzat al-wasl** (ٱ): skipped when joined; when starting, use fathah for ال, otherwise look at the **third letter**: dammah gives dammah, else kasrah.
- **Heavy letters**: خ ص ض غ ط ق ظ (*khuṣṣa ḍaghṭin qiẓ*)
- **Ra**: heavy with fathah or dammah, light with kasrah. Ra sakinah follows the vowel before it.
- **Lam of Allah**: heavy after fathah or dammah, light after kasrah.

## 7. Stopping (waqf)

| When you stop on… | You say |
|---|---|
| a vowel (ـَ ـِ ـُ) | a sukun |
| fathatan (ـً) | a long "aa" |
| kasratan / dammatan | nothing, the tanween is dropped |
| round ta (ـة) | a soft "h" |

## 8. Stop signs

| Sign | Meaning |
|---|---|
| مـ (ۘ) | Must stop |
| لا (ۙ) | Do not stop |
| ج (ۚ) | Either is fine |
| قلى (ۗ) | Stopping is better |
| صلى (ۖ) | Continuing is better |
| ∴ ∴ (ۛ) | Stop at one of the two, not both |
| س (ۜ) | Brief pause, no breath |

## 9. Marks on the page

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ـَ ـِ ـُ | Short a, i, u (1 count) |
| ـۡ | Sukun: no vowel |
| ـّ | Shaddah: doubled letter |
| ـً ـٍ ـٌ | Tanween: -an, -in, -un |
| ـٰ | Dagger alif: long "aa" |
| ـۥ ـۦ | Small waw / yaa after هُ / هِ |
| ـٓ | Madd sign: stretch beyond 2 counts |
| ـۢ ـۭ | Small meem: iqlab |
| ـ۟ | Small round zero: letter not pronounced |
| ۝ ۩ ۞ | End of verse · prostration · quarter-hizb |
